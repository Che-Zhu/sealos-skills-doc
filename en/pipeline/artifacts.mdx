All pipeline outputs are written under `.sealos/` in the target project directory.

```
<WORK_DIR>/.sealos/
├── config.json
├── template-references.json
├── template-references/
├── state.json
├── analysis.json
├── deployment-source/
│   ├── rendered.yaml
│   └── resource-map.json
├── topology-evidence/
│   └── <app-name>.yaml
├── build/
│   └── <service-key>/
│       └── build-result.json
└── template/
    └── index.yaml
```
